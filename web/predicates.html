Trusted Setup

Groth16 proves with per-circuit keys from a trusted setup, and none ships with this page. Each key starts from the published Hermez Powers of Tau (checked against its published hash) and gains one contribution per participant; proofs are sound if at least one contributor discarded their secret. Your contributions are made in this tab from fresh randomness that never leaves WebAssembly. Every key is checked against phase 1 before use, and key packs work unchanged with zk groth16 and snarkjs zkey verify. Verifiers accept proofs only under the setups they authorize below. This Groth16 implementation has not been independently audited.
